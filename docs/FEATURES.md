## Light / dark vs accent

- **Mode** (`AppsUseLightTheme` / `SystemUsesLightTheme`): overall light or dark chrome.
- **Accent** (DWM accent / colorization): highlight color, combinable with either mode.

## Software contrast

`SetDeviceGammaRamp` — not the panel OSD. Re-applied on a timer.

## CABC

`HKLM\...\GraphicsDrivers\CABCOption`: 0 Off, 1 Always, 2 Battery only.
